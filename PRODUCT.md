# Starship Product

## Register

product

## Users

Creators, community managers, designers, and casual users who want to turn a precise moment from a YouTube video into a polished GIF. They use the app in short, focused editing sessions and need to understand the workflow immediately without learning a full video editor.

## Product Purpose

Turn a YouTube link into a cropped, optimized GIF with direct control over timing, framing, size, motion, and quality. Success means users can move confidently from link to downloadable GIF, understand the effect of each choice, and avoid repeated trial-and-error renders.

## Brand Personality

Easily navigable, precise, playful, and creator-focused. The interface should feel capable and expressive while remaining calm enough for close visual work.

## Anti-references

Avoid overcomplicated professional-editor chrome, novelty interactions that hide standard controls, generic neon creator-tool styling, decorative motion that delays the task, and dense panels that expose every technical option at once.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next action unmistakable at every stage.
2. Keep precision controls close to the media they affect.
3. Reveal complexity only when the user asks for it.
4. Use playful feedback to celebrate progress, not decorate idle screens.
5. Explain quality and file-size tradeoffs in plain language.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide baseline accessibility through semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, useful labels, sufficient contrast, understandable errors, touch-friendly targets, and a reduced-motion fallback. Full WCAG certification is not a project requirement.
